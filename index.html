WHAT IS SWECOIN?

Swecoin is a Swedish digital project built around community, technology and culture. Inspired by Sweden's innovation, design and digital mindset, Swecoin brings together people who want to build something distinctive on the internet. The goal is simple: create a recognizable Swedish digital brand and a community that grows with it.
